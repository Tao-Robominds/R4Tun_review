\bigskip

To whom it may concern,

We thank the Associate Editor and reviewers very much for their feedback. We have revised the previous manuscript AUTCON-D-26-02407R titled ``R4Tun: LLM-guided adaptive segmental tunnel lining segmentation in point clouds'' to address the comments raised by the Associate Editor and reviewers. Please find our revised manuscript attached to this submission to the journal \textit{Automation in Construction}. We are submitting this paper as a revised and strengthened version of our earlier manuscript, prepared in direct response to the previous reviews.

In this revision, we have:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
  \item consistently clarified in the Abstract, Contributions, and Conclusions that R4Tun is a mechanism contribution for auditable, LLM-guided bounded parameter adaptation of an expert-designed pipeline, rather than a deployable final-inspection system;
  \item revised Figure~2 and the related descriptions to distinguish the four processing stages clearly and show the complete data-flow direction, while standardising stage labels across the other workflow figures;
  \item expanded the discussion of recent tunnel point-cloud research, including UnrollingNet and representative studies on point-cloud segmentation and defect detection; and
  \item re-audited the reference list by adding available DOIs, correcting the \LaTeX{} encoding of special characters and symbols, and removing redundant URLs where DOIs are provided.
\end{itemize}

Reviewer~1 confirmed that all previous comments had been addressed. A concise point-by-point account of the remaining revisions is provided in the accompanying Response to Reviewers. We also include both marked and unmarked versions of the revised manuscript.

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.

Thank you very much for your valuable processing of our work. We look forward to hearing from you on our revised manuscript.

\bigskip
\noindent Best regards,\\
Xinghui Tao, Zehao Ye, Guangming Wang, Jelena Ninić, Brian Sheil
